# Corine Land Cover — Copernicus data policy

Corine Land Cover (CLC) 2018 and earlier editions are products of the **Copernicus Land
Monitoring Service (CLMS)**, coordinated by the European Environment Agency (EEA). The French
national CLC database is produced by the SDES (Service des données et études statistiques,
Ministère de la Transition écologique) and is redistributed by IGN on the Géoplateforme
(WFS layer `LANDCOVER.CLC18_FR:clc18_fr`).

## Terms (summary — the official texts below are authoritative)

Copernicus data are made available on a **full, open and free-of-charge** basis under
Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) No 1159/2013 and Regulation (EU) 2021/696. Use,
reproduction, modification, distribution and commercial use are allowed, subject to:

- **attribution** of the source (see the notice below), and
- when the data have been modified, a statement that they have been modified
  (here: clipped to the FLAIR-HUB ROI extents and given `ROI_id` / `domain` columns).

There is no warranty, and the EU / EEA are not liable for any use of the data.

## Required attribution

>
